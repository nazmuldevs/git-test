Brewing Equipment

From first-apartment French presses to full espresso setups — every machine and brewer we sell behind our own counter, with the tips we give customers at checkout.

Semi-Automatic Espresso Machine

Machine

Best for: espresso, lattes & cappuccinos at home

Skill LevelAdvanced

A 15-bar pump with a built-in steam wand for pulling café-quality shots and texturing milk. Manual dosing and tamping give you full control once you've learned the machine.

Usage Tips

  • Preheat the portafilter and cup before pulling a shot
  • Dose 18g and aim for a 25–30 second extraction
  • Purge and wipe the steam wand after every use

Moka Pot (Stovetop Espresso Maker)

Machine

Best for: strong, espresso-style coffee without the machine

Skill LevelIntermediate

Aluminium stovetop brewer that pushes steam pressure through the grounds for a bold, concentrated cup. A classic that lasts decades with basic care.

Usage Tips

  • Fill the boiler chamber to just below the safety valve
  • Don't tamp the grounds — level them gently instead
  • Brew on low heat and remove as soon as it gurgles

Ceramic Pour-Over Dripper

Manual Brewer

Best for: clean, bright single cups

Skill LevelBeginner

A cone-shaped dripper that lets you control every part of the pour. Highlights the delicate, floral notes in light-roast and single-origin coffees.

Usage Tips

  • Rinse the paper filter with hot water before brewing
  • Bloom the grounds with a small pour for 30 seconds first
  • Pour in slow circles, keeping the water level steady
$28

French Press

Manual Brewer

Best for: full-bodied, rich coffee

Skill LevelBeginner

Immersion brewing with a mesh plunger for a heavier body and more oils in the cup. Forgiving to brew and easy to clean — a great first brewer.

Usage Tips

  • Use a coarse grind so grounds don't slip past the filter
  • Steep for 4 minutes before pressing
  • Press slowly and pour off right away to avoid over-extraction
$32

AeroPress

Manual Brewer

Best for: travel and fast, smooth cups

Skill LevelBeginner

A lightweight, nearly indestructible brewer that uses gentle pressure for a smooth, low-acid cup in under two minutes. Packs flat for travel.

Usage Tips

  • Use a medium-fine grind, similar to table salt
  • Try the inverted method for a fuller-bodied cup
  • Steep 1–2 minutes, then press steadily over 30 seconds
$30

Chemex

Manual Brewer

Best for: tea-like clarity and entertaining

Skill LevelIntermediate

An hourglass carafe with thick, proprietary filters that produce an exceptionally clean, sediment-free cup. Doubles as a table centrepiece.

Usage Tips

  • Only use genuine Chemex filters — they're thicker than standard cones
  • Grind medium-coarse to match the slower flow rate
  • Bloom for 30 seconds, then pour in three or four stages
$45

Cold Brew Maker

Manual Brewer

Best for: smooth, low-acid iced coffee

Skill LevelBeginner

A carafe with a removable steeping filter — no heat required. Long, slow extraction pulls sweetness forward while leaving bitterness behind.

Usage Tips

  • Use a coarse grind to keep the steeped coffee from turning muddy
  • Steep in the fridge for 12–18 hours
  • Dilute the concentrate 1:1 with water or milk over ice
$38

Ceramic Hand Grinder

Accessory

Best for: quiet mornings and travel

Skill LevelBeginner

A compact hand-crank grinder with ceramic burrs for a consistent grind, no outlet or cord required. Adjustable for everything from espresso to French press.

Usage Tips

  • Adjust the burr collar to match your brew method
  • Grind just before brewing for the freshest cup
  • Brush out leftover grounds weekly to prevent staleness
$45

Electric Burr Grinder

Accessory

Best for: households brewing daily

Skill LevelBeginner

A stepped-dial burr grinder that stores your favourite settings for pour-over, drip and espresso, so every brewer in the house gets the right grind fast.

Usage Tips

  • Start at the middle setting and adjust to taste
  • Keep the hopper away from heat and moisture
  • Empty the hopper if beans will sit for more than a week

Digital Scale with Timer

Accessory

Best for: dialing in a consistent brew

Skill LevelBeginner

A slim scale with a built-in timer for measuring coffee-to-water ratio down to the gram — the fastest upgrade to a noticeably better cup.
