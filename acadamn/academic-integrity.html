Academic Integrity

Support that respects your institution's rules

AcaDAMN provides educational and academic support designed to help you learn, plan, research and refine your own work — not to replace it.

Learning Support

Our materials are designed to help you understand a topic, not to be submitted as your own unaided work where that's against your institution's rules.

Original Work

Every piece of support is produced originally for you. Our specialists and editors do not fabricate data, citations, participants or results.

Responsible Use

Use AcaDAMN materials to guide your understanding, check your approach and strengthen your own writing — the responsibility for submission remains yours.

University Rules

Academic integrity policies vary by institution and course. Always check your own university's rules before using any external support materials.

Our position

AcaDAMN positions itself as a provider of educational and academic support — helping students with planning, research, referencing, editing and understanding their own coursework. Materials produced through AcaDAMN are intended as study aids and models, not as a substitute for a student's own learning and submission obligations.

What we ask of every student

Before using any support material, please check and follow your own institution's academic integrity, plagiarism and assessment policies. If you are ever unsure whether a particular type of support is appropriate for a specific assessment, ask your tutor, supervisor or academic support office first.

What we ask of every specialist and editor

Our specialists and editors are expected never to fabricate data, citations, research participants, results or experiments, and to produce original material tailored to each student's brief.

Questions about this policy

If you have a question about how this applies to your situation, contact our support team before placing an order.
