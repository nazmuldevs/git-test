How does AcaDAMN match me with a specialist?
We match your subject, academic level and brief to a specialist with relevant experience in that area.
How is my price calculated?
Pricing depends on service type, academic level, word count and deadline. Use the calculator on our pricing page for an instant estimate.
Is my information kept confidential?
Yes. Your personal details and files are kept confidential and are never shared beyond what's needed to deliver your support.
What referencing styles do you support?
We support Harvard, APA, OSCOLA, Vancouver and other major UK academic referencing styles.

Yes — every support level includes revision requests within your service window. See our Revision Policy for details.

We encourage every student to check their own institution's academic integrity policy — read more on our Academic Integrity page.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept major debit/credit cards through a secure, PCI-compliant payment provider.
Can I message my specialist directly?
Yes, through your customer dashboard once your order is confirmed.
