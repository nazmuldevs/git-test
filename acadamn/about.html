About AcaDAMN

Academic support, built around how students actually work.

AcaDAMN exists to give UK students clear, honest academic support — subject specialists, useful free resources, and pricing you can understand before you commit.

Our mission

Studying is hard enough without confusing pricing, vague processes or support that doesn't understand your subject. AcaDAMN was built to make academic support feel straightforward: match with a specialist in your field, understand your price upfront, and get guidance shaped around your actual brief.

What AcaDAMN provides

We provide personalised academic support across assignments, essays, dissertations, research and editing — alongside free tools, guides and sample resources that any student can use, whether or not they order support.

How support works

Every request is reviewed and matched to a specialist with relevant subject experience. You can message your specialist, track progress through your dashboard, and request revisions within your service window. See the full breakdown on our services page.

Quality standards

Support passes through a structured quality review before delivery, covering structure, referencing accuracy and clarity against your brief.

Academic integrity

We're clear that students remain responsible for following their own institution's academic policies. Read our full approach on the Academic Integrity page.

Confidentiality

Your personal details and files are kept confidential and are never shared beyond what's required to deliver your support.

Support channels

Our support team is reachable by email, WhatsApp and Facebook — see the contact page for details.

Have a question before you get started?

We're happy to talk through your brief with no obligation.
