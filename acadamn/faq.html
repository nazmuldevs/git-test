Before Ordering

How do I get started with AcaDAMN?
Use the instant quote calculator on our pricing page to get an estimate, then continue to share your full requirements.
Do I need to sign up before I get a quote?
No — you can get an instant estimate without creating an account.

Services

What types of academic support do you offer?
Assignment, essay, dissertation, coursework, research and editing support — see our full services page.
Can you support postgraduate and PhD-level work?
Yes, our specialist network covers undergraduate through to PhD level.

Pricing

How is my price calculated?
Pricing depends on service type, academic level, word count and deadline — see our pricing page for a full breakdown.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. Your quoted price is the price you pay unless you add extras yourself.

Experts

How are specialists matched to my project?
We match based on your subject, academic level and the specifics of your brief.

Delivery

How fast can I get support?
Turnaround depends on your deadline setting, from urgent 48–72 hour requests to 2+ weeks.

Files

Can I upload a marking rubric or reading list?
Yes, file upload is available as part of the order requirements step.

Payments

What payment methods are accepted?
We accept major debit/credit cards through a secure, PCI-compliant payment provider. We never store raw card numbers.

Revisions

Can I request revisions after delivery?
Yes — every support level includes revision requests within your service window.

Confidentiality

Is my personal information kept private?
Yes. Your details and files are confidential and never shared beyond what's needed to deliver your support.

Academic Integrity

How does AcaDAMN approach academic integrity?
We provide educational and academic support; you remain responsible for following your institution's rules. Read our full Academic Integrity policy.

Account

Do I need an account to order?
An account is created as part of checkout so you can track your order and message your specialist.
