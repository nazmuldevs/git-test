Our Story

Started on one street corner. Still tastes like it.

Bean Boutique opened as a five-table shop with a single roaster and a big idea: coffee tastes better when it's shared. Over a decade later, we still roast in small batches, greet regulars by name, and treat every cup like it's for a friend.

Now we're bringing that same warmth online — so wherever you're brewing from, you're part of the neighbourhood too.

  • Direct-trade beans from farms we've visited ourselves
  • Roasted weekly in-house, never sitting on a shelf
  • Equipment we actually use behind our own counter
